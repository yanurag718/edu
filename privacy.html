Privacy Policy

Learn how Eduvanta Learning handles your information.

At Eduvanta Learning, we respect your privacy. This page explains the types of information that may be collected when you use our website and how that information may be used. Please review this policy and update it to reflect your actual practices before publishing.

01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Eduvanta Learning website and its educational services. By using our website, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. Where consent is legally required, we will request it separately.

02 Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact details you submit through forms.
  • Account information required for student or mentor access.
  • Course enrolment and learning-related information.
  • Messages or enquiries you send to us.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and pages visited, where applicable.

We should only collect information that is necessary for our services.

03 How We Use Information

Information may be used, where applicable, to:

  • Provide and manage educational services and courses.
  • Create and maintain student accounts.
  • Respond to questions and provide support.
  • Maintain website security and improve performance.
  • Send important service-related communications.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

04 Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain functionality, understand website usage, or remember preferences. The actual cookies used depend on the services enabled on the website.

If analytics, advertising, or other optional tracking tools are enabled, disclose them here and provide appropriate choices where required.

05 Sharing of Information

We do not claim to share information with any particular provider unless that provider is actually used. Information may be processed by service providers supporting website hosting, communications, course delivery, or security, where applicable.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect the security and rights of users and the service.

06 Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may link to external platforms, such as video-hosting or educational services. Those platforms operate under their own privacy policies. Please review their policies before providing personal information to them.

07 Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

08 Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including legitimate educational, security, and legal requirements. Actual retention periods depend on the type of information and applicable law.

09 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Contact us using the details below to submit a privacy request. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10 Children's Privacy

Our educational services should be used in accordance with applicable age requirements. If we learn that personal information has been collected from a child in a manner that violates applicable law, appropriate steps should be taken to address it.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website, services, or legal requirements change. The revised version should be published on this page with an updated effective date.
